Make a custom parts bin on your laser cutter

Bin Parts generates the flat cut pattern for an open-front storage bin sized exactly to your shelf, drawer or workbench. Every part is sized from your measurements and material thickness, so the slots line up with the stock you actually have. It runs in your browser with no sign-up.

How it works

  1. Enter your sizes. Type the length, width and height of the bin and the thickness of your sheet. Use the IN / MM switch for metric.
  2. Check the 3D preview. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. The model updates as soon as you change a number.
  3. Download the SVG. You get one file with every part: back, bottom, two sides and the angled front.
  4. Cut and assemble. Open the file in your laser or CNC software, cut, and slot the parts together. A little wood glue makes it permanent.

Cutting tips

  • Measure your material. Sheet goods are rarely their nominal size. "1/4 inch" plywood is often closer to 0.21–0.23 in. Enter what your calipers say.
  • Check the scale before cutting. After importing, confirm a part's size against your numbers in LightBurn, Inkscape or your CAM software and scale if needed.
  • Do a test cut. Cut one slot pair in scrap first to dial in kerf and fit.
  • Good materials: Baltic birch plywood, MDF, hardboard and acrylic all work well.

Recommended materials & tools

What you need to cut and assemble your bins:

  • Baltic birch plywood sheets Strong, flat and laser-friendly. The go-to for shop bins.
  • MDF sheets Cheap, consistent thickness, great for prototypes.
  • Cast acrylic sheets For clear or colored bins you can see into.
  • Digital calipers Measure your real sheet thickness before you generate.
  • Wood glue A few drops in the slots makes the bin permanent.
  • Laser masking tape Prevents scorch marks on the faces of your parts.
  • Honeycomb laser bed Cleaner cuts with less flashback on the back side.

Frequently asked questions

Is the bin generator free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no cost. Enter your sizes and download the SVG.

What software can open the SVG file?

Any program that imports SVG, including LightBurn, Inkscape, Glowforge, xTool Creative Space, LaserGRBL, Adobe Illustrator and most CNC CAM software.

What material thickness should I enter?

Measure your actual sheet with calipers and enter that number. Plywood sold as 1/4 inch or 3 mm is often thinner than its label, and the slots need to match the real thickness for a good fit.

Can I design in millimeters?

Yes. Use the IN / MM switch next to Select Measurements and the fields convert automatically.
